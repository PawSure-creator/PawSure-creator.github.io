What PawSure does

  • Collects dog and cat food recall and safety notices published by public authorities: the U.S. FDA, the Government of Canada, the EU's RASFF, the UK Food Standards Agency and France's RappelConso.
  • Lets you choose your region (North America or Europe), filter by dog or cat food, and search by brand or product.
  • Links every notice to its official source, and reminds you to check the manufacturer's website for lot numbers and dates.
  • Sends recall alerts for new official notices in the region you choose: North America (U.S. FDA, Government of Canada) or Europe (EU RASFF, UK Food Standards Agency, France's RappelConso).
  • Keeps your pets and photos on your phone. No account, no ads, no subscription: PawSure is a one-time purchase.

Important

PawSure is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting on behalf of any government agency, regulator or pet food manufacturer. Recall information comes from public sources and may be incomplete or delayed. Always check the official notice, and contact your veterinarian if you are worried about your pet's health.
