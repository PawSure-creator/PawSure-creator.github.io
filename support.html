Common questions

I don't get recall alerts

Check that notifications for PawSure are allowed in Android settings, and that you chose an alert preference on the Saved screen. Alerts cover new official notices for the region you chose: North America (U.S. FDA and Government of Canada) or Europe (EU RASFF, UK Food Standards Agency and France's RappelConso).

A recall from the alert says "Recall not available"

The authority may have updated or withdrawn the notice. Use "Open official notice" to see the original.

How do I stop alerts and remove my device from the alert service?

Turn off PawSure notifications in Android settings, or uninstall PawSure. Registration data for a device that has not checked in with PawSure for more than 12 months is deleted automatically. See the privacy policy for details.

Is this veterinary advice?

No. PawSure shows public recall information. If you are worried about your pet's health, contact your veterinarian.
